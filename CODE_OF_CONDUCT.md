# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, respectful, and constructive community where contributors can collaborate safely and productively.

## Our Standards

The following standards outline expected behavior within this project community.

### Be Respectful

- Treat all contributors with respect and dignity.
- Be mindful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Avoid dismissive, hostile, or aggressive communication.

### Be Professional

- Provide constructive feedback and criticism.
- Focus on the technical or project issue rather than the individual.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to correct them.
- Follow project guidelines and maintain professional communication in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

### Be Considerate

- Recognize that contributors have different levels of experience.
- Be patient with newcomers and help them learn.
- Respect people's time and effort when requesting changes or reviews.
- Focus on what benefits the community and project as a whole.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Request changes in behavior when necessary.
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation in the project when violations occur.

Moderation decisions will aim to be fair, transparent where possible, and focused on protecting the health of the project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository
- Issue trackers
- Pull request discussions
- Project documentation
- Community forums or chat channels related to the project

It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces. Examples include:

- Using an official project email address
- Posting from official project social media accounts
- Acting as a representative of the project at events or conferences.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

Reports should be sent to:

**mgifford@users.noreply.github.com**

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will make every effort to maintain the confidentiality of reporters.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may apply the following consequences depending on severity and context:

**1. Correction**

A private written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate and requesting a change in conduct.

**2. Warning**

A formal warning issued for repeated or significant violations. Continued violations may lead to stronger consequences.

**3. Temporary Ban**

Temporary removal from participation in project discussions or contributions for a defined period.

**4. Permanent Ban**

Permanent removal from participation in the project community.
